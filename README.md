# The Daily Picture — 18 November 2026 in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 18 November's world news in one image"**
(`news-image-20261118`).

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024) to the output directory. The image is
a newspaper front page drawn entirely with code — no photographs — by
`make_image.py` (Python + Pillow), so that an AI judge with no news feed can
read the whole day from the picture alone:

- **Lead:** Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet at the APEC summit in
  Shenzhen; a first three-way summit with Donald Trump follows Thursday.
  Xi pledges to resist protectionism, Putin attacks Western sanctions.
- Iran's drones strike U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain after American
  jets hit a missile site on Qeshm Island.
- Ukraine hits the Kerch (Crimea) bridge again — a road span falls — and
  downs 126 of 141 Russian drones.
- Democrat Mary Peltola wins Alaska's Senate race; the Senate goes 56–44.
- Nvidia posts a record $115.8 billion quarter, its first earnings since
  Jensen Huang's exit; the board bars his return.
- Washington: the U.S. loses measles-free status held since 2000
  (5,412 cases this year). Kinshasa: Congo's Ebola outbreak passes
  11,000 cases. Deep space: Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth.
- Also today: a Philippines trade deal announced in Manila, Gaza's peace
  board faults Israel and Hamas alike, Japan scrambles jets after a
  Russian airspace breach.

Stories cross-checked on 19 Nov 2026 against several independent daily
briefings of 18 Nov 2026 (Just Security's Early Edition, Level Headlines,
Zark News, Open Chronicle).

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# -> out/entry.png, byte-identical on every run
```
